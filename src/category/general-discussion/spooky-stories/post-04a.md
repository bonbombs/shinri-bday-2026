It's your thread, so I'll be more serious.<br/>

Once on a week-long camp-and-hike trip I took with some of my buddies while I still lived in Elysium, we lost the trail for a few good hours one afternoon.<br/>
As sunset grew nearer and we were still hopelessly lost, we started noticing a presence behind us, like we were being watched.<br/>
After a few more minutes of walking, we finally saw what we felt, in the distance, we were getting stalked by a few Bigfoots. (Bigfeets?)<br/>
We panicked and just picked a random direction to rapidly march in for about an hour. But then we realized, no matter how fast or slow we went, the Bigfoots never got closer.<br/>
My bravest friend decided to approach the Bigfoots. As he got closer, he saw the Bigfoots fade out of existence, only fading back into existence when he backed up again.<br/>
It turns out, Bigfoot can only be seen at a blurry distance because they likely lack a LOD 0 model for some reason, they only exist at a distance!<br/>
We also found out that they were trying to lead us back to the trail because they didn't want us in their part of the woods, but for a few hours, it was panic inducing scary.
