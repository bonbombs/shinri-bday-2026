i have a reoccurring nightmare in which i can hear her weeping in terror on the other side of a cave in<br/>
so i dig and dig and claw my way through the debris until my hands are bleeding<br/>
but the cave in is always slightly deeper the rubble slightly thicker<br/>
her voice slightly further away<br/>
so i keep going and pile rocks and dirt around me and risk destabilizing the cave even further<br/>
but theres still more rubble theres more material between us and shes further away still still crying still terrified<br/>
i can never reach her before i wake up
