once upon a time, there was a little fish, and it swam around and around in a big fishbowl, showing its marvelous colors.  <br/>
the humans always loved the little fish because it was so bright and cheery.  <br/>
but one day, the humans did not appear.  <br/>
or the next.  <br/>
or the next.  <br/>
soon the little fish became cold and hungry. <br/>
it got harder and harder to breathe.  <br/>
algae climbed up the walls.  <br/>
and then one day, there was no more little fish swimming in the tank.  <br/>
and the little fish never swam again.
