Scholars are divided as to whether this was a long standing historical measurement error or if it’s actually a major change that’s happened recently.<br/>
It doesn’t affect maritime activities of course, as all of the boats use only the top few meters of sea and all of the crabbing and shellfish harvesting happen in the unchanged shallower waters.<br/>
But given part of the plausibility of Rissuna’s existence largely was based around the idea that Rissun Bay was just too vast to be accurately surveyed in its entirety, alongside the “photographical and eye witness evidence” of course, this measuring change makes it far more likely that Rissuna should be more easily found or sighted with modern technology.
