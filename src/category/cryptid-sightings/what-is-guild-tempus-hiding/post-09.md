Yeah, ice creatures are exceedingly rare, as most are actually creatures with the elemental typing of ice. (See: the pod of ice whales that was sighted off the coast of Xenokuni like a herd of icebergs)
Really the weird bit is that the icklefish has the fish elemental typing, but that is its charm!

The only proper ice monster that I can name off the top of my head is the Yuki-onna, which is not a cryptid because it’s been recorded in combat encounters, has known drops, and has a very specific but predictable spawn pattern.<br/>
On the flip side, a very similar Xenokunian monster that is likely a cryptid is this long dark haired lady in a white dress that acts like a ghost, but can’t be repelled by holy magic or properly attacked at all, if the rumors are true at least.
