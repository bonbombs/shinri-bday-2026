in my teenage years i got caught in evening fog with some of my friends <br/>
it creeped up on us so quickly that within minutes we couldnt see each other anymore <br/>
all i could see was fog and small glowing lights that i thought were fireflies <br/>
i started hearing them call for me but everytime i got closer to them their voices seemed to come from somehwere else

i didnt know it at the time but that was my first encounter with the apali<br/>
they had me stuck with them for almost 2 entire days before a cryptid hunter finally found me and managed to lead me out<br/>
its what inspired me to start up photography and create more awereness about all the the different kinds of cryptids that exist

i know that the glowing lights weren't fireflies but sometimes i still get unnerved when i see them and i now know to check the weather reports before i leave home
