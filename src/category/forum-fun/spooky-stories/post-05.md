I read a story where a man made bets with this creature in the forest near his house. As long as he won the bets, whatever he bet was fine and he got something (money, a promotion at work, good health) in exchange.

What he bet affected how good what he got from it was. The bets weren't hard and he'd never lost one. One day he bet his wife and kids and he lost the bet for the first time.

The creature disappeared and any trace his family ever existed disappeared with it.
