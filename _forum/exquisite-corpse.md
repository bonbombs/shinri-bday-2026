let’s play a writing game!!!! let’s write something together!

for exquisite corpse, there’s a bunch of ways to play but this thread is for playing it this way:

A: writes a sentence
B: writes a sentence based on A
C: writes a sentence based on B

where we spoiler the sentences to keep it so you only look at the person’s right before you!

don’t add a sentence twice in a row and after after 5ish sentences feel free to say that corpse is finished if you wanna start a new one!
